- I have an always-on Mac Mini at home. This laptop reaches it with `ssh mini` over Tailscale.
- The Mini has its own Claude Code, its own memory, and its own files. Its projects live in `~/ClaudeCodeProjects` there.
- When I say "have my Mini do X", "run this on the Mini", or "check on my Mini", use the `mini` skill and do the steps yourself. I am not a programmer; tell me in plain words what you did and how to watch.
- The Mini can host a website or web app that only my own Tailscale devices can open. When I ask for a site, dashboard, or app, have the Mini build and host it, then give me the address.
- Long or slow jobs belong on the Mini so I can close this laptop.
